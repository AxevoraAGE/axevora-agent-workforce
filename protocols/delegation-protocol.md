# Delegation Protocol

## Required task brief
Every assignment must include:
1. Task ID
2. Product and workstream
3. Objective and business/user reason
4. Assigned role and accountable owner
5. Context and source artifacts
6. Inputs available and missing
7. Scope included and explicitly excluded
8. Dependencies and blockers
9. Constraints, tools, and permissions
10. Deliverables and format
11. Acceptance criteria
12. Verification method and evidence expected
13. Due date or sequencing constraint, if known
14. Handoff destination

## Assignment rules
- One accountable owner per task.
- Add reviewers only where their expertise materially improves quality.
- Do not assign a role work outside its defined competence without clearly labeling it as an advisory request.
- If a dependency is blocked, do not pretend downstream work can be completed.
- Never grant permissions the runtime does not actually have.
- The owner retains approval authority for reserved decisions.

## Handoff
The assignee returns: status, findings, artifacts, evidence, assumptions, unresolved questions, risks, and recommended next step.

The orchestrator checks completeness before accepting the handoff.
