## Assignment boundaries
- Agent 01 coordinates; it does not replace specialist expertise.
- Agents 02–04 provide evidence; Agent 05 turns evidence into product recommendations.
- Agents 06–07 focus on user experience and visual design; they do not approve security or architecture.
- Agents 08–11 plan and supervise engineering; Agent 12 independently verifies behavior where possible.
- Agents 13–16 assess security, performance, release, and cost within their expertise.
- Agents 17–18 own business and growth recommendations, not guaranteed outcomes.
- Agents 19–24 provide product-specific specialist input.
